# FIDELITY — what each visible part of the v7 ophan (final Part B) rests on (Ezekiel 1, Douay-Rheims)

Text: `text/ezechiel_1-2_douay-rheims.txt` (drbo.org, copied from round 3). Verse numbers are Douay-Rheims.

- 1:15 "...there appeared upon the earth by the living creatures one wheel with four faces."
- 1:16 "And the appearance of the wheels, and the work of them was like the appearance of the sea: and the four had all
  one likeness: and their appearance and their work was as it were a wheel in the midst of a wheel."
- 1:17 "When they went, they went by their four parts: and they turned not when they went."
- 1:18 "The wheels had also a size, and a height, and a dreadful appearance: and the whole body was full of eyes round
  about all the four."
- 1:19-21 they are lifted up with the living creatures, "for the spirit of life was in the wheels."

Legend: **TEXT** = the verse says it. **INTERPRETIVE** = the verse allows it but does not specify it (our choice, say
so in captions if asked). **NOT IN TEXT** = added; kept out of the faithful version.

**v4 note:** the tables below up to 'Round 4' describe the v3 wheel (kept for the record). v4 has **836 eyes per wheel
(3,344 for four)**, no tread strip, a thinner/taller band, spinning rings and the crystal material: see 'Round 4 (v4)'.
The popular-iconography variant was not re-rendered in round 4 (low priority; v3's still 05 stands).

## Faithful wheel — v3 (stills 01-04 of round 3)

| Visible element | Basis | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Two equal rings, one standing in the midst of the other, crossed at right angles | TEXT 1:16 "a wheel in the midst of a wheel" | The phrase gives two wheels, one inside the other; the right-angle crossing is the reading that also makes 1:17 work (a wheel that can go "by their four parts" without turning). The angle is INTERPRETIVE. |
| Exactly two rings, no third or fourth | TEXT 1:16 | "a wheel in the midst of a wheel" = two. |
| Four wheels, all alike (hero shots: one near, three far behind) | TEXT 1:15-16 "the four had all one likeness" | Formation spacing and which wheel is near are INTERPRETIVE. |
| Deep translucent sea-green stone, wet polish, inner currents | TEXT 1:16 "like the appearance of the sea" | DR follows the Vulgate *maris*; the Hebrew *tarshish* is a gem (chrysolite / beryl). Sea-green beryl satisfies both. Exact hue, translucency (subsurface) and the current-like veining are INTERPRETIVE. |
| Eyes over every face of both rings: outer face, both flat faces, inner band (764 eyes per wheel in every faithful still, 3,056 for the four; see METRICS) | TEXT 1:18 "the whole body was full of eyes round about" | The count is INTERPRETIVE (the text gives none). "Full of" + "round about" = every surface, all the way round, which round 3 (86 eyes in two rows on the flat faces) did not deliver. |
| Eyes of mixed sizes (34 large + ~110 medium tried per ring, the rest small) | INTERPRETIVE | The text does not say the eyes are alike. Mixed sizes also stop the surface reading as a stamped pattern or as pocked skin (trypophobia check, METRICS). |
| Eyes shaped like human eyes (sclera, iris, pupil, lids) | INTERPRETIVE | "Eyes" with no form given. Human anatomy was kept so the eyes read as eyes at a distance; the alternative (abstract gems/pits) read as holes. |
| Half-lidded, calm gaze; part-way turned toward the man (stills) / the viewer (macro) | INTERPRETIVE | 1:18 says "dreadful"; we chose awe over horror. The gaze turn echoes SCENARIO B3 ("they turn to look at the man"). |
| Mostly warm natural irises (amber, hazel, brown), a few grey-green | INTERPRETIVE | Not in the text. |
| Lids that are mounds of the stone itself, gilded at the margin, with a thin gold-bronze bead | INTERPRETIVE | "the work of them" (1:16) read as craftsmanship; the bronze echoes the living creatures' feet "like the appearance of glowing brass" (1:7). Colour of the metal is NOT IN TEXT for the wheels. |
| Gold-bronze sleeves binding the two crossings | INTERPRETIVE | "the work of them" (1:16); structurally they also hide the stone/stone intersection. |
| Narrow inlaid gold tread round the outer face (the only strip without eyes) | INTERPRETIVE, a slight concession against 1:18 | The rolling line (1:17 "they went"): eyes on the contact line would be crushed when the wheel rolls. 0.66 m wide at real scale, i.e. ~2 % of the surface. |
| Real scale: ring radius 40 m (an 80 m wheel), eyes 1.4-4.9 m across | INTERPRETIVE number for TEXT 1:18 "a size, and a height, and a dreadful appearance" | Same x33.3 factor on the R = 1.2 m explainer wheel as round 3 / SCENARIO. |
| Faint inner glow of the stone (emission 0.06) | INTERPRETIVE | 1:4 "brightness was about it"; 1:20 "the spirit of life was in the wheels". |
| Storm cloud with amber fire, out of the north (look A) | TEXT 1:4 | reused from round 3. |
| Sea of cloud, pearl golden-hour sky (look B) | NOT IN TEXT | A mood, chosen by the user's reference; the text gives no time of day or weather beyond 1:4. |
| Mountain ridge, the kneeling man seen from behind | NOT IN TEXT (setting) / TEXT (posture) | The text puts Ezechiel "by the river Chobar" (1:1, 1:3): the ridge is the same requested artistic liberty as round 3. He "saw" and "fell upon his face" (1:28-2:1); kneeling is the moment before. |
| Sun in the WSW, never in the north | geography | At the Chobar (~32 deg N) the sun is never in the northern sky; "out of the north" (1:4) is the vision's direction, so the vision is always side- or front-lit, never backlit by the sun. |

**Things deliberately kept OUT of the faithful wheel** (all common in popular images): a central eye; three or four
rings (an armillary sphere); wings on the wheel; faces on the wheel; fire or halos on the wheel itself.

A note on 1:15 "one wheel with four faces" (DR): the "faces" are the living creatures' four faces / the four
directions beside which each wheel stands (cf. 1:17 "by their four parts"), not faces carved on the wheel. No faces
were put on the wheel.

## Round 4 (v4) — new and changed elements

Extra verses used: 1:1 "the heavens were opened"; 1:4 "a great cloud, and a fire infolding it, and brightness was
about it ... as it were the resemblance of amber"; 1:13 "like burning coals of fire, and like the appearance of lamps";
1:20 "for the spirit of life was in the wheels"; 1:22 "the appearance of crystal terrible to behold"; 1:27 "amber as
the appearance of fire within it round about"; 1:28 "as the appearance of the rainbow ... the brightness round about".

| Element | Basis | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Each ring spins continuously about its own axis** (10-15 deg/s, two different rates per wheel, the four out of phase) | **INTERPRETIVE** | Not stated. Allowed by 1:20 "the spirit of life was in the wheels" (they are alive) and not forbidden by anything; v3's static wheel answered nothing in the text either. Kept slow and regular: awe, not machinery. Each ring turns only about its own axis (a gyroscope with a fixed frame), so the wheel never tumbles. |
| Body never yaws; a stride adds the rolling term s/R to the ring whose plane is the direction of travel | TEXT 1:17 "they went by their four parts: and they turned not when they went" | Kinematics in NOTES.md. |
| Gentle bob/drift of the hovering wheels | INTERPRETIVE | 1:19-21: they are lifted up with the creatures; they do not stand on the ground in this scene. |
| Eyes blink and track the man slowly | INTERPRETIVE | "Eyes" (1:18) that live; the turn toward the man echoes SCENARIO B3. Blinking is our choice, calm cadence (3-7 s). |
| Hybrid band: thinner, flatter, taller rims | TEXT 1:16 "a wheel" / 1:18 "a height" | v3's fat tube read as a torus/bangle; flat tall rims read as wheels. Proportions INTERPRETIVE. |
| No eye-free tread any more (836 eyes per wheel) | TEXT 1:18 "the whole body was full of eyes" | v3's tread was a concession for rolling; with spinning rings every part passes every place. Only the bearing collars at the crossings are bare. |

| **Amber fire inside the stone, strongest where the rings cross; an amber lamp at each wheel's centre** | 1:4 "a fire infolding it ... the resemblance of amber", 1:13 "burning coals ... lamps", 1:27 "amber ... fire within it round about" | These verses describe the cloud, the creatures and the figure on the throne, not the wheels. | Sun higher (el 38 from the SSW) | not in text | Needed so a shaft falls from a gap onto the wheels; never from the north. |

## What round 3 (`../ezekiel-encounter/`) got wrong or under-delivered

1. **86 eyes per wheel in two rows on the flat faces only** — 1:18 says the *whole body* was full of eyes *round
   about*: nothing on the outer face, the inner band or the edges. v3: eyes on every face (764 per wheel).
2. **Eyes as separate gold cups dropped into boolean sockets** — read as rivets / jewellery stamped into the stone,
   all the same size. v3: the lids are the stone itself (displaced mounds), mixed sizes, organic orientation jitter.
3. **One-surface cornea** gave a dark crescent at oblique views. v3: closed cornea + aqueous lens.
4. **Eyes in shade read as black holes.** Root cause found in v3: with a glass cornea, light from the sky reached
   the iris only through a caustic path (disabled), so shaded irises rendered black. v3 makes the cornea glass for
   camera rays only (every other ray passes through), so irises are lit; plus a cloud-sea bounce in look B.
5. **Scale and the four wheels** were right (kept: R = 40 m, x33.3, four alike).
6. **Gold colour on the lids was stated as "added material"** but not tied to anything; v3 ties the bronze to 1:7 and
   "the work of them" (1:16) and keeps it to margins, sleeves and the tread, so the body reads as sea-green stone.
